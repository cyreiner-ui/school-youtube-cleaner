NAME
School YouTube Cleaner

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Keeps YouTube classroom-friendly by hiding selected recommendations and promotional elements and turning autoplay off.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
School YouTube Cleaner is a simple classroom-focused extension. On YouTube, it hides:
- right-side recommended/related videos on watch pages
- end-screen video suggestions and cards
- shopping, merchandise, and promotional modules
- Explore and Trending navigation links

It also attempts to keep YouTube autoplay turned off.

It intentionally leaves the YouTube homepage, search, Shorts, comments, video title, channel information, description, captions, and normal playback controls available.

The extension has no user settings, no analytics, no advertising, and no external network service. All changes are performed locally in the browser.

CATEGORY
Productivity

SINGLE PURPOSE
Reduce distracting or inappropriate recommendation surfaces while a teacher uses YouTube for classroom playback.

DATA USE
No user data is collected, transmitted, or sold.
